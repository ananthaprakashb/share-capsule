When the fake news is an image or video

Screenshot or poster

Do not upload it here until the site can extract and search its content reliably.

  1. Copy the visible headline or Tamil text.
  2. Paste that text in Message / Article.
  3. Search the same image with Google Lens or Bing Visual Search.
  4. Check the earliest date and original caption.

Video or reel

Paste the public link and describe the exact scene being claimed.

  1. Add the timestamp.
  2. State the claimed place and date.
  3. Capture one clear frame.
  4. Reverse-search that frame to find older appearances.
