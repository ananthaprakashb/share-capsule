# Safety, privacy, and limitations

ShareCapsule tools are intended to assist users, not replace official authorities, licensed professionals, or primary evidence.

## General principles

- Treat automated results as guidance rather than guarantees.
- Verify time-sensitive information with the original official source.
- Do not upload unnecessary sensitive documents or private media.
- Do not rely on evidence scores as proof that a claim is true or false.
- Review third-party privacy terms before opening external search, media, or sharing services.

## Browser-local processing

Some features, including supported screenshot OCR, are designed to run locally in the browser. Browser-local processing reduces server exposure but does not eliminate risks from the user device, browser extensions, or external links.

## External services

ShareCapsule may link to official sites, fact-check publishers, search engines, reverse-image tools, media platforms, and social applications. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.
