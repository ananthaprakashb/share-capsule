# Shareable content

ShareCapsule presents audio, video, cards, ideas, and utilities in a format designed for simple mobile access and sharing.

## Typical capabilities

- Browse public releases and tools.
- Open a release detail view.
- Play supported embedded audio or video.
- Copy a share message or use the device share sheet.
- Share through WhatsApp and other supported applications.

## External platforms

Some releases may link to or embed third-party platforms such as YouTube or Spotify. Availability and playback behavior depend on those providers.
