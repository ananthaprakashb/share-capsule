ShareCapsule birthday collection

Birthday wishes for everyone

Choose from 50 Canva-generated birthday cards. Add the recipient’s name and a short message, then send the selected card through WhatsApp or open it in Canva.

On supported phones, “WhatsApp” shares the card image through the system share sheet. When image sharing is unavailable, it opens WhatsApp with the selected Canva card link and your message.
