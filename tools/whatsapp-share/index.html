Share the clips

Tap a clip preview to open the original YouTube video. Use Share clip to open the device share sheet, or download each file and add it to WhatsApp Status.

Private by design: your selected video is processed locally with FFmpeg WebAssembly. ShareCapsule does not upload the video file to its servers.
Important: a standalone MP4 file cannot contain a clickable web hyperlink inside WhatsApp Status. The generated clips link to YouTube when viewed on this ShareCapsule page; for WhatsApp, share or paste the YouTube URL separately with the status.

YouTube allows creators to download videos they uploaded, but downloading other users’ videos is not generally provided as a file-download feature. Use only media you own or have permission to reuse.
